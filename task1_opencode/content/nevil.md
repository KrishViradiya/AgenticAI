## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI can produce a working UI in minutes, but "working" and "pleasant to use" are not the same thing. It still needs a human eye for design, accessibility and the small details users notice.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Building software will feel more like directing than typing. Taste, user empathy and clear communication will matter more than knowing a framework.

## My hot take on AI
