## Team name
The Prompt Engineers Who Can't Center a Div

## Our shared view on AI and the future of software engineering
AI agents are already good teammates for boilerplate, tests and first drafts, but they still need humans who understand the system, set the direction and check the result. We expect the job to shift from writing code to specifying, reviewing and verifying it. The engineers who do well will be the ones who can explain clearly what they want and notice quickly when the answer is wrong.
