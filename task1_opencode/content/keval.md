## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI is great for scripts, configs and debugging error messages, but it happily suggests insecure shortcuts. Security and operations still need a human who understands the risks.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Agents will deploy, monitor and fix systems on their own, so the real job will be setting the guardrails: permissions, tests and rollback plans.

## My hot take on AI
Giving an AI agent production access without a sandbox is just chaos engineering with extra steps.
