## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI assistants are great at boilerplate and first drafts, but they still need someone who understands the system to check their work. They speed me up most when I already know what I want.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Engineers will spend less time typing code and more time specifying, reviewing and testing what agents produce. Knowing *why* something should be built will matter more than knowing the syntax.

## My hot take on AI
The best prompt engineer on your team is just the person who writes the clearest tickets.
